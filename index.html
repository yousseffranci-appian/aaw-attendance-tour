Attendance at Work on Appian

A guided tour of sickness absence management: manager, Occupational Health, HR, leaders and staff.

Every absence managed, every step on record

Follow Daniel, 88 days off sick with no Occupational Health referral, through his manager, OH, HR and the Trust's leaders, and see his own view on his phone. Every screen is the real Appian application, following the Trust's attendance policy.

Click the pulsing dot or press → to move on. All people and data are fictional; Pennine Vale is a fictional trust. Emails are simulated. Every AI output is labelled, and a person approves anything that leaves the system.
